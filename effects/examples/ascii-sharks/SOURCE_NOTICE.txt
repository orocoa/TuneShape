ASCII Shoal is an independent implementation based on the user-owned Ariadne / TuneShape ASCII Wave character-sampling and flow-field methods. Fish geometry, movement and the standalone module were written for this implementation. The previous third-party adaptation is retained only as local history and is excluded from this runtime and public source package.
